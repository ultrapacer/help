## Following Additional Athletes

Follow additional athletes (regardless of current/forecasted position/place) using the button below the Leaderboard.

<img width="423" alt="Screenshot 2025-06-27 at 8 38 59 AM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d841daf0-2de9-4a83-b6db-3e02d667f196" />

Look up the athlete by bib number.

<img width="423" alt="Screenshot 2025-06-27 at 8 39 44 AM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2bf15cc2-7a96-4c3b-81cd-677071eb15e8" />

Select the athlete with the checkbox and click the Track button to add them to the Additional Athletes table.

The Additional Athletes table is controlled by the same Forecast/Current results toggle as the Leaderboard.

<img width="680" alt="Screenshot 2025-06-27 at 8 41 57 AM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/32bfc3ce-1e27-4dde-8862-6557f2a9148a" />

To **stop following an athlete**, use the trash button in the table to remove the row.

Last and next check-ins for additional athletes will show up in the waypoint cards (regardless of current/forecasted position/place).

Highlight an athlete in the Additional Athlete table to see that athlete’s:
- highlighted bib in the map and elevation profile
- past check-ins, paces, and forecasted future check-ins throughout the waypoint cards (denoted by a highlighted bib number)
