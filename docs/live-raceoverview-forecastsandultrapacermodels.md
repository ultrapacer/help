## Forecasts and ultraPacer Models

**ultraPacer mMdels**

ultraPacer contains models for courses, event conditions, and athletes.

- The course models account for factors like distance, elevation change, grade, and terrain.

- Event conditions such as daylight and heat impact are also factored in.

- Athlete performance in Race Overview and Runner Mode primarily consists of the overall change in normalized pace (change in effort when course and event conditions are factored out).

**Forecasts**

ultraPacer applies course and condition models to an athlete’s split data to forecast future waypoint arrivals and split times.  As more check-in data becomes available for an athlete, performance trends are analyzed and make the forecasts more and more accurate. Trends include the overall change in pace over the race, the impact of darkness, impact of heat, and so on.  The most recent check-in times are most heavily weighted in forecasts.

*Early forecasts are based on limited data - forecasts may change more between early waypoints and should stabilize over the course of the event.*
