## Leaderboards

View data for the top 10 of the selected field as either forecasted results or current progress.

**Field**

<img width="249" alt="Screenshot 2025-06-27 at 8 25 39 AM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a582d61e-cc55-4d4c-b6ff-e597ebf28295" />

Toggle between the Overall, Men’s, and Women’s top 10 runners.  These runners will be displayed:
- as rows in the Leaderboard
- as bibs on the elevation profile and map
- as rows in the check-in tables within the waypoint cards

**View Results**

<img width="180" alt="Screenshot 2025-06-27 at 8 27 58 AM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/710b3420-22f0-44b0-8719-f89cb6d2a3fd" />

Toggle between the **Forecast** and **Current** Leaderboards to view different data about the selected field.

**Forecast Leaderboard**

<img width="673" alt="Screenshot 2025-06-27 at 8 28 46 AM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a3eade21-e49f-498b-ac28-39772a69e070" />

The table shows, in order, an athlete’s:
- Forecasted finish position/place (within selected field)
- Gender, bib number, and Name
- Current position/place (within selected field)
- Difference between current and forecasted finish position/place (within selected field)
- Forecasted finish time

*Forecasts are italicized.*  Confirmed finish times (denoted by unformatted text and a checkmark) will replace forecasts as they become available.

The table shows, in order, an athlete’s:
- Current position/place (within selected field)
- Gender, bib number, and Name
- Last waypoint checked into
