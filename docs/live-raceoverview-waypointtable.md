## Waypoint Table

The waypoint table is composed of **waypoint cards** and the **segments** between the waypoints.

**Waypoint Cards**

<img width="431" alt="Screenshot 2025-06-27 at 8 46 50 AM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2891b9b0-de18-4881-b845-b4c5291d1b6a" />


- <img width="432" alt="Screenshot 2025-06-27 at 8 47 43 AM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4f1d10af-45ba-4717-a7c2-b08640a21133" />

    * The top of the waypoint card lists the mileage location of the waypoint, its name, and the number of the waypoint on the course.

- <img width="188" alt="Screenshot 2025-06-27 at 8 48 36 AM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/81ee71ea-fbe3-4bd5-8f4e-fd771e39a7be" />

    * The icons on the left show what athletes have access to at the waypoint (e.g. full aid, crew, drop bags, etc.).  See the Legend for a description of each.

    * If there’s a cutoff time at the waypoint, it’s listed next to the hourglass icon below the other features of the waypoint.

- <img width="316" alt="Screenshot 2025-06-27 at 8 49 23 AM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/fa8e29d8-b1f2-4178-9351-20b2668c8512" />

    * The table on the right of the card shows check-ins at that waypoint.  The table lists athletes by bib number and name, shows check-in time, and the source of the check-in time.

*ultraPacer forecasts are denoted by the ultraPacer logo and italicized text.*

Official check-in times replace ultraPacer forecasts as they become available (if available at that waypoint); these are donated by a checkmark and roman (unformatted) text.
