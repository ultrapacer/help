#### Pace Chart

![Pace chart](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c2df5f1f-217d-4486-b669-8e3e3f8d50ea)


ultraPacer shows a **runner’s change in pace over the course of a run** in the pace chart below the elevation profile - this is estimated based on mathematical models for the course and event that are applied to the runner’s waypoint check-in times.

The x-axis shows distance and the y-axis represents the pace in distance/time.  Change the units for the axes in the controls.
