## Controls

ultraPacer is a customizable tool.  Display the data that you want and how you want it.

#### Unit Display

Units are applied to instances across the page - from the course description to the elevation profile to the waypoint table and beyond.

**Distance** can be viewed as either miles or kilometers

<img width="65" alt="Screenshot 2025-06-06 at 8 38 39 AM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e17c2dbb-9031-49aa-ab8a-43219ca45205" />

**Elevation** can be viewed as either feet or meters

<img width="107" alt="Screenshot 2025-06-06 at 8 53 14 AM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/453e1e64-040f-44ca-8dc8-868aa1ba4955" />

**Pace** can be viewed as minutes per mile or miles per hour
