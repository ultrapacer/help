## Link an Athlete

#### Athlete Lookup

If ultralive splits are available for the event, link an athlete to autopopulate their waypoint check-ins and forecasted arrival times in the waypoint table.

<img width="696" alt="Screenshot 2025-06-06 at 11 57 51 AM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bb7c9e74-87ed-4420-b60e-25036cf1ec9b" />

Use the green **Link Athlete** button to get started. 

<img width="402" alt="Screenshot 2025-06-06 at 11 43 49 AM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7da7cdb0-c964-46d1-8978-0eb2c62fe2c2" />

**Search for your athlete** by bib number or name.

![Athlete look up](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/30b0ceb3-0456-46bf-8bb4-61b6d8ffa49b)

Select your athlete and click the blue **Track** button.

<img width="696" alt="Screenshot 2025-06-06 at 12 03 35 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9e2a5e25-97d1-4610-bee1-2b64f9ef2f48" />

The athlete's bib number will now be displayed over the waypoint table and their check-ins and forecasted arrival times will autopopulate.

#### Change Athlete

<img width="696" alt="Screenshot 2025-06-06 at 12 03 35 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1f2a489f-8713-47f9-b29b-5f5983eb4329" />

If you want to change the athlete that you're tracking, use the white **Edit** button to bring up the **Athlete Lookup** window.
