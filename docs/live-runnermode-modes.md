## Mode 

#### Overview

You are currently in **Runner Mode**.  View, edit, or add check-in times for your athlete in the waypoint table.  ultraPacer forecasts future waypoint arrival times and your runner’s estimated current location based on their past check-ins.  

If live splits are available, track your athlete by **linking their bib number** - their split times will be automatically added to the waypoint table.

Switch to **Race Overview Mode** using the button at the top of the page to view the leaderboards for this event (if available).

#### Forecasts and ultraPacer models

ultraPacer forecasts arrival times using models to account for course and event factors - such as terrain difficulty, elevation change, heat, altitude, and more.  These models are applied to a runner’s live splits* (from ultralive or manual entry in the waypoint table) to project future arrival times.

*Recent segment durations and waypoint arrival times are more heavily weighted in the models when forecasting times*.*
